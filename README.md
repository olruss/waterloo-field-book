# Waterloo Field Book

A personal home-search dossier: 132 freehold Waterloo, Ontario homes (3+ bedrooms, up to $900k) from a single realtor.ca snapshot on 6 Oct 2026, scored and ranked, with a photo review of the top 12.

This is the public edition. It leaves out listing photos, seller descriptions and negotiation notes. Prices are asking prices. There are no sold comparables, and nothing here is financial or real-estate advice.

The page is one self-contained `index.html` and is not indexed by search engines.
